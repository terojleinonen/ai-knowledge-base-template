# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

## 1. Working hours
Core hours are 10:00–15:00 Helsinki time. Outside core hours employees may organise their own schedule. The standard working week is 37.5 hours.

## 2. Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to four days per week. At least one day per week must be spent at the Tampere office, normally Tuesday, which is the team day. Working abroad is allowed for a maximum of 30 days per calendar year and must be approved by your manager at least two weeks in advance.

## 3. Vacation
Full-time employees earn 2.5 vacation days for each full month of employment, which amounts to 30 days per year. At least 12 days must be taken as a continuous summer holiday between June 1 and September 30. Unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days.

## 4. Sick leave
Notify your manager before 9:00 on the first day of illness. A self-certification is accepted for up to three consecutive days; a doctor's certificate is required from the fourth day onwards.

## 5. Equipment
Every employee receives a laptop of their choice (MacBook Pro or a Linux ThinkPad) and a home-office budget of 600 euros, renewed every three years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## 6. Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 euros for courses, conferences and books, plus five paid learning days per year.

## 7. Parental leave
Northwind Labs pays full salary for the first 8 weeks of parental leave for all parents, in addition to the statutory Kela allowance.
