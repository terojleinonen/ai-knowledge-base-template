# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook 2026

Valid from 1 January 2026. This handbook replaces the 2024 edition. Where a team agreement or your employment contract says something different, your contract applies.

## 1. About Northwind Labs

Northwind Labs designs wireless sensors for commercial buildings. Our main product is the Aurora Sensor Platform. We have about 140 employees in three offices: Tampere (headquarters), Helsinki and Berlin. The working language is English; Finnish and German are welcome in local office matters.

## 2. Working hours

The standard working week is 37.5 hours. Core hours are 10:00–15:00 in the local time of your office. Outside core hours you organise your own schedule with your team. Hours are recorded in Harvest by the end of each week.

Meetings should not be scheduled outside core hours unless everyone attending agrees. Fridays after 13:00 are kept free of internal meetings ("quiet Friday afternoon").

## 3. Overtime

Overtime must be agreed with your manager in advance. Overtime is compensated as time off: each overtime hour earns 1.5 hours of time off, taken within six months. Overtime is not paid out as money, except for on-call work (see section 4). Work on public holidays earns 2 hours of time off per hour worked.

## 4. On-call duty

Engineers in the Platform and Cloud teams take part in a weekly on-call rotation. On-call starts on Monday at 10:00 and lasts one week. The on-call compensation is 350 euros per week, plus 1.5 times the hourly rate for time spent handling incidents outside working hours. Nobody is on call more than one week in four. Swaps are arranged in the #oncall Slack channel and recorded in PagerDuty.

## 5. Remote work

You may work remotely up to three days per week. At least two days per week are spent at your home office, and one of them is your team's office day. In Tampere the shared office day is Tuesday; in Helsinki it is Wednesday; in Berlin it is Thursday.

Working abroad is allowed for up to 60 days per calendar year, in periods of at most 30 consecutive days. Working abroad must be approved by your manager and People Operations at least three weeks before travel, because of tax and insurance rules. Working from outside the EU and EEA additionally needs approval from the security team.

## 6. Vacation

Full-time employees earn 2.5 vacation days for each full month of employment, which amounts to 30 days per year. The vacation year runs from 1 April to 31 March.

At least 12 vacation days must be taken as a continuous summer holiday between 2 May and 30 September. Unused vacation days can be carried over to the next vacation year, up to a maximum of 15 days. Days above that limit are lost unless your manager agreed in writing to postpone them.

Vacation requests go through Personio at least four weeks in advance for periods longer than one week, and one week in advance for shorter periods.

## 7. Sick leave

Notify your manager before 9:00 on the first day of illness. You can self-certify sickness for up to five consecutive days. A doctor's certificate is required from the sixth day onwards. Occupational health care is provided by Terveystalo in Finland and by our insurer in Germany.

If your child under 10 is sick, you may stay at home for up to four days per illness with full pay.

## 8. Parental leave

Northwind Labs pays full salary for the first 12 weeks of parental leave, for all parents, in addition to the statutory allowance (Kela in Finland, Elterngeld in Germany). Parental leave can be taken in up to four separate periods before the child turns two.

## 9. Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop of their choice: a MacBook Pro or a Lenovo ThinkPad with Linux. Laptops are replaced every three years, or sooner if they fail. You also receive a home-office budget of 800 euros, renewed every three years, for a desk, chair, monitor or similar equipment.

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours (see the security policy). Equipment remains the property of Northwind Labs and is returned on your last working day.

## 10. Phone and internet

Northwind Labs pays for a work phone subscription with unlimited data in your country of residence. Home internet is reimbursed up to 40 euros per month against a receipt.

## 11. Learning and development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 2,000 euros for courses, conferences, certifications and books, plus five paid learning days per year. Conference travel is paid from the learning budget only when you are not presenting; speakers' travel is paid by the company. Unused learning budget does not carry over.

## 12. Performance and development reviews

Development reviews take place twice a year, in March and in September. Before each review you write a short self-assessment in Personio. Salary reviews are held once a year, in March, and changes take effect from the April salary.

## 13. Bonus scheme

All permanent employees are part of the company bonus scheme. The bonus is paid in March and depends on the company's annual revenue and profit targets. The target bonus is 8 percent of annual base salary, and the maximum is 12 percent. Employees who joined during the year receive a pro-rata bonus. Employees whose notice period is running in March do not receive a bonus.

## 14. Wellbeing

Every employee receives a sports and culture benefit of 400 euros per year (ePassi in Finland, Urban Sports Club in Germany). Massages at the Tampere office are subsidised: the company pays half of a 45-minute massage, at most once a month.

## 15. Lunch

Lunch benefit is provided in Finland with Edenred, 12.75 euros per working day. In Berlin, a lunch allowance of 7.50 euros per office day is paid with the salary.

## 16. Travel and expenses

Work travel and expenses follow the separate Travel and Expense Policy. Book travel through Northwind's travel agency, Matkahelmi, and submit expenses in Personio within 30 days.

## 17. Code of conduct

We treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. Report concerns to your manager, to People Operations, or anonymously through the whistleblowing channel at https://northwind.example/speakup. Reports are handled by an external provider and the company's ethics committee.

## 18. Side jobs

Side jobs are allowed if they do not compete with Northwind Labs and do not affect your work. Tell your manager about any side job before you start it. Working for a competitor or a customer always needs written approval from the CEO.

## 19. Notice periods

During the first six months of employment, either party may end employment with 14 days' notice (trial period). After the trial period the notice period is one month for employees with less than five years of service and two months after that, unless your contract says otherwise.

## 20. Leaving Northwind Labs

On your last working day, return your laptop, phone, access badge and YubiKey to IT. Your accounts are closed at the end of that day. An exit interview with People Operations is offered to everyone who leaves.
