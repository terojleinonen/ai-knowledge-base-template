# Office Guide

Practical information about Northwind Labs' offices. Questions: office@northwind.example.

## Tampere (headquarters)

Address: Hatanpään valtatie 34, 33100 Tampere, 4th floor. Open on weekdays 7:00–19:00 with your access badge; at other times the building's alarm must be turned off with your PIN code.

The office has 70 desks, of which 20 are fixed desks for the hardware lab teams and the rest are hot desks. Book a hot desk in the Officely app if you plan to come on Tuesday, the shared office day, when the office is busiest.

Meeting rooms on the 4th floor: Näsijärvi (12 people), Pyhäjärvi (8), Pispala (6), Hervanta (4) and two phone booths. The hardware lab is on the 3rd floor and needs separate access, requested from the lab manager.

Parking: the office has 12 parking spaces in the courtyard, booked through the #parking-tampere Slack channel on a first-come basis. Two of the spaces have electric car chargers. Bicycle storage is in the basement.

Lunch: the building's restaurant Ravintola Hatanpää serves lunch on weekdays 10:30–13:30. Your Edenred benefit card works there.

## Helsinki

Address: Bulevardi 21, 00180 Helsinki, 2nd floor, in a shared office run by Sponda. 25 desks, all hot desks. The Helsinki team's office day is Wednesday.

Meeting rooms: Lauttasaari (8 people) and Suomenlinna (4). Larger rooms in the building can be booked through the Sponda app.

Parking: there is no parking for employees at the Helsinki office. The nearest public garage is Q-Park Forum; parking fees for customer visits are reimbursed.

## Berlin

Address: Oranienstraße 185, 10999 Berlin, 3rd floor. 20 desks. The Berlin team's office day is Thursday. The Berlin office opened in September 2024 and mainly hosts the Cloud team and the sales team for German-speaking countries.

Meeting rooms: Spree (8 people) and Tempelhof (4).

Parking: no parking spaces; use public transport. The office pays half of the Deutschlandticket for employees in Berlin.

## Visitors

Register visitors in advance in the Envoy app. Visitors are met at reception and must be accompanied in the office. Visitors use the NW-Guest Wi-Fi network.

## Pets

Dogs are welcome at the Helsinki office on Fridays, if they are calm and house-trained and nobody in the team is allergic. Dogs are not allowed at the Tampere office because of the hardware lab, or at the Berlin office under the building's rules.

## Emergencies

In an emergency, call 112. First aid kits and defibrillators are next to the reception in each office. Fire wardens are listed on the noticeboard in the kitchen.
