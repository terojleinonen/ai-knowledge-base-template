NORTHWIND LABS EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Edition 2024. Valid from 1 January 2024.

1. WORKING HOURS
The standard working week is 37.5 hours. Core hours are 9:00-15:00 Helsinki time. Hours are recorded in the timesheet spreadsheet at the end of each month.

2. OVERTIME
Overtime must be agreed with your manager in advance. Overtime is paid with the next salary at 1.5 times the hourly rate. Alternatively, it can be taken as time off hour for hour.

3. REMOTE WORK
Employees may work remotely up to four days per week. At least one day per week must be spent at the Tampere office, normally Tuesday. Working abroad is allowed for a maximum of 30 days per calendar year and must be approved by your manager at least two weeks in advance.

4. VACATION
Full-time employees earn 2.08 vacation days for each full month of employment, which amounts to 25 days per year. At least 12 days must be taken as a continuous summer holiday between June 1 and September 30. Unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days.

5. SICK LEAVE
Notify your manager before 9:00 on the first day of illness. A self-certification is accepted for up to three consecutive days; a doctor's certificate is required from the fourth day onwards.

6. PARENTAL LEAVE
Northwind Labs pays full salary for the first 8 weeks of parental leave for all parents, in addition to the statutory Kela allowance.

7. EQUIPMENT
Every employee receives a laptop of their choice (MacBook Pro or a Linux ThinkPad) and a home-office budget of 600 euros, renewed every three years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

8. LEARNING BUDGET
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 euros for courses, conferences and books, plus three paid learning days per year.

9. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Performance reviews are held once a year in November. Salary changes take effect from the January salary.

10. BONUS
The company bonus is paid in March. The target bonus is 5 percent of annual base salary.

11. WELLBEING
Every employee receives a sports and culture benefit of 250 euros per year through ePassi.

12. LUNCH
Lunch benefit is provided with Edenred, 11.50 euros per working day.

13. NOTICE PERIODS
The trial period is four months. After the trial period the notice period is one month.
